2️⃣  MATHEMATICAL MODEL IMPLEMENTATION

   ✓ Elliptical Fire Spread Physics
     Formula: Area(t) = (π/2) × R_flank × (R_head + R_back) × t²
     
     Components:
     • R_head: Head spread rate (velocity in wind direction)
     • R_back: Back spread rate (against wind, 10-15% of head)
     • R_flank: Flank spread rate (perpendicular, 30-40% of head)
     • t: Time since ignition (minutes)
     
     Calibrated rates by vegetation type:
     - Pine forest: 8-18 m/min head rate
     - Garrigue/Maquis: 10-24 m/min head rate
     - Grassland: 5-12 m/min head rate
     - Mixed forest: 6-14 m/min head rate

   ✓ Escape Probability Threshold Model
     Formula: P_escape = 1 / (1 + exp(-(Area_ha - 3)))
     
     • Critical threshold: ~3 hectares
     • Below 3 ha: High containment probability
     • Above 3 ha: Exponential escape risk increase
     • Wind-adjusted threshold: 3 + 0.1 × (wind_kmh - 20)

   ✓ Risk-Adjusted Impact Calculation
     Effective_Ha = ΔArea_direct + ΔP_escape × Historical_Escaped_Size
     
     Example: 
     • Direct area saved by acceleration: 15.6 ha
     • Escape probability reduction: 0.05
     • Historical escaped fire size: 150 ha
     • Total effective ha saved: 15.6 + (0.05 × 150) = 23.1 ha

   ✓ Carbon Emissions (IPCC Tier 1)
     tCO2/ha = Fuel_Load × Combustion_Frac × 0.47 × (44/12)
     
     Example:
     • Garrigue fuel load: 12 t/ha
     • Combustion fraction: 0.50
     • Factor: 0.47 × (44/12) = 1.72
     • Result: 12 × 0.50 × 1.72 ≈ 10.3 tCO2/ha

   ✓ Economic Value Conversion
     € Avoided = Area × (Suppression_Cost + Asset_Value)
     
     Mediterranean (High-risk):
     • Suppression: €2,200-2,500/ha
     • Asset value: €6,500-8,000/ha
     • Total: €8,700-10,500/ha
     
     Central France (Low-risk):
     • Suppression: €1,800/ha
     • Asset value: €4,500/ha
     • Total: €6,300/ha
