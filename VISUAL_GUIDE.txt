PART 2: HOW THE CALCULATION WORKS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

THE FIRE GROWTH MODEL
──────────────────────

    Standard Response (45 min)          Early Detection (30 min)
    
    Fire ignition (t=0)                Fire ignition (t=0)
         ↓                                   ↓
         │  Slow growth                     │  Slow growth
    T=10 │  ~2.5 ha                     T=10 │  ~2.5 ha
         │                                   │
    T=20 │  ~10 ha                      T=20 │  ~10 ha
         │                                   │
    T=30 │  ~22 ha ← Firefighters       T=30 │  ~22 ha ← Early detection!
         │  start traveling              │
         │                                   │  Firefighters arrive
    T=45 │  ~47 ha ← Firefighters       │
    [END]│  arrive & suppress            └─→ 30 min: Fire contained at ~22 ha
         │
    [OUTCOME] Fire spread to 47 ha      [OUTCOME] Fire contained at 22 ha
                                        
    AREA SAVED: 47 - 22 = 25 hectares

BUT THERE'S MORE...

THE ESCAPE THRESHOLD EFFECT
────────────────────────────

The model considers: Once a fire reaches ~3 hectares, containment gets MUCH harder.

Without Early Detection:
    Fire reaches 3 ha at t=7 min
    → Escape probability: 80%
    → If it escapes: Grows to 150-300 hectares
    → Expected size: (20% × 47 ha) + (80% × 200 ha) = 170 ha

With Early Detection:
    Fire only reaches 2 ha at t=30 min
    → Escape probability: 10%
    → If it escapes: Grows to 150 ha
    → Expected size: (90% × 22 ha) + (10% × 150 ha) = 35 ha

RISK-ADJUSTED IMPACT:
    170 ha (expected without) - 35 ha (expected with) = 135 hectares!

This is why the chart shows a jump at 15+ minutes—you're preventing escape.

PART 5: KEY INSIGHTS FROM THE SCIENCE
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

INSIGHT 1: Non-Linear Benefits
────────────────────────────────

5 min saved:  5.9 ha
10 min saved: 11.1 ha (NOT 2×, only ~2× because of fire growth)
15 min saved: 35.1 ha (NOT 3×, MUCH more due to escape threshold)
20 min saved: 43.7 ha (only slightly more)
30 min saved: 173.4 ha (massive jump when fire definitely contained)

Lesson: There are "sweet spots" where small time savings have huge impacts.

INSIGHT 2: Wind Speed Amplifies Impact
───────────────────────────────────────

15 min detection, Dept 06:

Wind 15 km/h:  15.6 ha saved
Wind 25 km/h:  15.6 ha saved (baseline)
Wind 35+ km/h: 35.1 ha saved (2.2× multiplier)

Lesson: Early detection is MOST valuable during high-wind events (when damage
would be worst anyway).

INSIGHT 3: Regional Variation
──────────────────────────────

Same 15-min detection, different regions:

Mediterranean (Dept 06):  €368,726 value protected
Central France (Dept 63): €228,233 value protected

Lesson: Mediterranean coast = higher stakes (dense population, high property
values). ROI on early detection systems higher there.

INSIGHT 4: Escape Threshold Dominates
──────────────────────────────────────

Fire below 3 ha:
  → Can be contained by 1-2 fire engines
  → 90% containment rate

Fire above 3 ha:
  → Requires multiple resources
  → 10% containment rate

Early detection → Keep fire below 3 ha → Huge containment advantage
